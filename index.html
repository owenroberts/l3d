one, two, three, four

by owenribbit ~

click on the ⃞ to view the animation for each composition.

"one, two, three, four" is an album of experimental compositions using the procedural generation program GREG (Guided Realtime Euphoney Generator) aka "doodoo".

GREG works by adding modulations to the rhythm, melody, harmony and effects of a composition that acculumate with each play.

the number next to each track is the number of times the composition will play and modulate. you can adjust this number to change the length of the album play back.
